Tokenization is the first step of text processing. The tokenizer splits the text
into tokens: words, numbers, punctuation marks and special symbols.

Consider the sentence "Hello, world! Price = 12.50$". A simple whitespace tokenizer
keeps "Hello," as one token, while a better tokenizer separates the comma.
Constructions like COVID-19, C++, mother-in-law and don't are harder to handle...
