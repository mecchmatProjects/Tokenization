A neural network is a model that consists of layers of neurons. Each neuron
computes a weighted sum of its inputs and applies an activation function.

Training a neural network means finding weights that minimize the loss function.
The learning rate is usually small, for example 0.001 or 0.01. With 1,000 training
examples and 10 epochs the model sees 10,000 examples in total.

Deep learning models need a lot of data and a lot of computation. Is it worth it?!
For many tasks, yes: deep learning models are the best models for images and text.
